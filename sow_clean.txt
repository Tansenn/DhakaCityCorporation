Background, Purpose and Objectives Gazipur Zila Parishad proposes a modular digital platform to improve administrative records, internal approvals, HR processes, project monitoring, meetings and management reporting. This revised scope also expressly includes citizen-facing Dakbungalow E-Booking for four designated Dakbungalows, rather than leaving booking as a future optional module. The system shall complement, not replace or bypass, mandatory government platforms or statutory approval authority.
- Digitize agreed registers, workflow steps, status tracking, and management reporting.
- Enable citizens to discover, request and pay for bookings of approved Dakbungalow facilities.
- Provide role-based dashboards, auditable approvals, secure payment confirmation and downloadable booking permits.
- Deliver a controlled, supportable solution with three years of technical maintenance after operational acceptance. 2.
